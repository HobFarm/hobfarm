# HobFarm Research

Build the evidence needed for an entertaining and trustworthy article while preserving the author's own knowledge as a visible source class.

Read [`../../../docs/editorial/EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md`](../../../docs/editorial/EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md). Use [`references/evidence-states.md`](references/evidence-states.md) when claims have mixed provenance or external corroboration is incomplete. Use [`references/source-ledger.md`](references/source-ledger.md) when the piece has more than a few consequential claims.

## Start from the article's questions

Identify the central mechanism, dates and quantities that carry the story, current facts, disputed points, author-supplied identifications, visual evidence, calculations, and useful primary records.

Treat the author's direct identification, observation, photograph, experiment, and recollection as evidence with explicit provenance. Seek external corroboration in proportion to the consequence of error. A low-stakes caption identification can carry author provenance; medical, legal, financial, safety, and similarly consequential claims call for stronger support.

## Research with receipts

Prefer primary records for exact dates, specifications, official behavior, direct statements, and original artifacts. Use strong secondary reporting and scholarship for context and competing interpretations. Use community and anecdotal sources for experience, language, behavior, and leads with their role clearly stated.

Use current sources for facts with a short shelf life. Record retrieval dates where they matter. Inspect images, maps, charts, scans, captions, timestamps, legends, and underlying data when visual material supplies evidence.

Research can corroborate, qualify, complicate, or contradict the starting idea. Preserve the original author claim in the notes, update the public wording to the supported state, and report material changes.

## Stop at useful sufficiency

Research is ready for composition when the central mechanism has support, key dates and quantities have receipts, important uncertainty is understood, obvious counterevidence has been checked, visual evidence is identified, and remaining gaps can be framed accurately.

Keep the research packet smaller than the article it supports. Store durable article research under `research/<article-slug>/`; keep transient searches and scratch output outside the repository.
