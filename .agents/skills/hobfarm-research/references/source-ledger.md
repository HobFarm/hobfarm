## Receipt detail

For a receipt that needs more context, record:

- claim ID
- source title and publisher
- primary, secondary, community, personal, or visual source type
- publication or record date
- retrieval date for time-sensitive material
- URL, file, page, figure, frame, timestamp, or map reference
- concise paraphrase
- what the source establishes
- limits or conflicting evidence
- useful visual or data opportunity

## Calculations

Record inputs, units, sources, formula, conversions, result, sensitivity to uncertain assumptions, and an ordinary-language comparison.

## Author provenance

Record author-confirmed identifications, firsthand observations, photographs, recollections, and confidence markers that the public copy relies on.

## Open questions

Keep unresolved items that still affect the article's central mechanism, factual accuracy, captioning, or visual plan.
