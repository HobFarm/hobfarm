# HobFarm Prose

Create finished prose that sounds like the author took the time to notice, investigate, and explain the subject.

Read [`../../../docs/editorial/EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md`](../../../docs/editorial/EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md). Use [`../../../docs/regular-prose-guidelines.md`](../../../docs/regular-prose-guidelines.md) as the final sentence-level pass. For substantial drafting or voice revision, read [`references/voice-modes.md`](references/voice-modes.md) and one or two current article examples nearest the task.

## Preserve the source field

Treat rough chat as working source material. Separate its memories, observations, claims, questions, connections, jokes, phrases, media ideas, and production requests before smoothing the surface.

Carry forward:

- the author's confidence and uncertainty
- concrete physical and technical details
- irritation that reveals a mechanism or broken promise
- humor with timing or explanatory value
- profanity that carries the author's real emphasis
- odd connections with a demonstrable bridge back to the subject
- approved copy at its approved level of revision

Use first person for what the author saw, did, tried, photographed, remembered, paid, built, misunderstood, or changed their mind about. Ask for missing personal material with a compact marker when it materially matters, then continue the work around it.

## Build the article around material

Open near a real object, scene, behavior, image, number, place, or remembered moment. Let chronology, cause, dependency, comparison, scale, incentive, adaptation, uncertainty, and consequence move the reader through the piece.

Give each paragraph useful work: evidence, mechanism, scene, complication, scale, connection, joke, or consequence. Let the ending return to an object, image, contradiction, number, question, or joke that means more after the journey.

Length follows the material. Structure follows the article's dominant engine. Headers, sidebars, quotations, figures, and interactive elements serve the reader rather than a fixed template.

## Finish for publication

Make facts accurate at their stated evidence level. Keep speculation legible and lively. Strengthen nouns, verbs, dates, places, measurements, artifacts, and visual opportunities. Read for natural rhythm and a speaking voice with control.

Return the requested brief, outline, draft, revision, caption, or final copy. Keep research notes and implementation instructions outside the public prose unless the chosen article form calls for them.
