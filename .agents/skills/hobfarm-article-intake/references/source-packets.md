# Source Packet Reconciliation

Use this structure when an attachment combines author instructions, AI planning, research claims, media, and repository work.

## Current outcome

State what the user wants accomplished now.

## Source classes

Classify material closely enough to preserve its authority and editorial use:

- **Author authority and approval:** current author direction; approved copy or structure; rejected direction.
- **Author evidence and voice:** recollection; author-confirmed fact or identification; opinion or judgment; uncertainty; question; joke, aside, or phrasing.
- **Evidence work:** checkable fact; supplied source or receipt; unresolved research question.
- **Proposals and production:** proposed connection; AI proposal; production instruction; selected media.

These are interpretive classes, not a requirement to tag every sentence. One passage may perform more than one job, but its authority and evidence state must remain clear.

Newer explicit author direction supersedes older AI or working direction. Preserve enough superseded and rejected history to explain consequential changes and stop discarded ideas from silently returning. AI suggestions never gain author authority merely because they are repeated, polished, or copied into later working documents.

## Author direction

Capture the author's thesis, taste, personal knowledge, approved decisions, selected media, phrases, jokes, uncertainty, and desired changes.

## Approved material

Identify finished copy, captions, assets, and decisions that already have approval. Record the permitted revision depth when it is stated.

## Proposed route

Place ChatGPT-created outlines, optional ideas, suggested research, visual concepts, and implementation steps here. Preserve their usefulness while keeping their proposal status visible.

## Repository reconciliation

Inspect every material claim about paths, schemas, routes, components, commands, scheduling, deployment, and current site behavior. Record the current equivalent and whether the packet describes an existing state or a requested change.

## Evidence work

List claims that need corroboration, author confirmation, visual comparison, calculation, or current research. Preserve author identifications and recollections in their stated evidence class while that work proceeds.

## Selected actions

Choose the research, prose, visual, build, and editorial-mesh nodes actually needed for this task. Let each node receive only the source material and decisions it needs.

## Durable placement

Keep temporary attachments as task inputs. When a packet remains useful, store its reconciled form under `research/<article-slug>/` with provenance and status in the filename or header. Move accepted article copy into `src/content/articles/` and durable project policy into the appropriate project document or skill.
