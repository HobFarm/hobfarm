## Deliberately unresolved

- No online cast list or program directly naming Denberg in a specific Stardust edition was located.
- The full legal-name spelling remains inconsistent. “Dietlinde Ortrun Zechner” is common; “Ostrun” appears to be a copied error.
- The au-pair job, Paris audition, marriage timeline, and later occupations rely on secondary biography until primary records are inspected.
- The original 1968 *Midnight* and 1972 *San Francisco Examiner* pages were not inspected. Sensitive claims attributed to them stay out of the article body except as examples of how the later narrative was built.
- No current proof of life was located, and absence of an obituary is not proof.
- The Playboy pages supplied in chat are not present as workspace files, so page-number and file-level derivative work remains deferred.
