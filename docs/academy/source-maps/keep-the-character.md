## Evidence boundaries

Supported:

- identity, wardrobe, style, scene, and motion must be recorded as separable roles;
- a later sheet may infer unseen clothing, and the inference must not become an identity fact;
- the blonde and green subjects provide same-look/different-base evidence;
- controlled comparisons make continuity drift easier to inspect.

Not yet supported:

- one universal provider prompt;
- exact buttons or settings across current model interfaces;
- perfect cross-model identity consistency;
- a clean first-time learner result without operator knowledge;
- the exact correction sequence for a documented failed generation.
