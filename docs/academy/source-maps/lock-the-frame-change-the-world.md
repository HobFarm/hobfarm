The public program supports transformation classification, locked visual anchors, comparison, and clear treatment of documentary versus invented results. It does not make an undocumented reconstruction factual.

## Lesson outline

1. Classify the job as documentary, restoration, alternate history, or fictional transformation.
2. Inspect rights and source evidence.
3. Lock subject, perspective, camera, geometry, and visible anchors.
4. Define the change in time or condition.
5. Generate or composite the transformation.
6. Inspect failed frames and drift.
7. Label factual and invented context separately.
8. Publish a pair, sequence, or transition film.

## Media requirements

- source and result at matching dimensions;
- visible anchor overlay;
- one failure comparison;
- provenance/invention label example;
- optional transition film with poster, captions, transcript, and static pair.
