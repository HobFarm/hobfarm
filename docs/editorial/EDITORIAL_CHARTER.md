# HobFarm Editorial Charter

## Publication promise

HobFarm turns curiosity into an entertaining, informative, visually engaging expedition. A piece may begin with a photograph, object, memory, irritation, interface, place, artifact, calculation, or strange connection, then follow the trail until the larger mechanism becomes visible.

The finished work should reward attention. Facts are supported where accuracy matters. Firsthand knowledge is credited. Recollection keeps its original confidence. Inference shows its bridge. Speculation is recognizable and allowed room to be imaginative. Satire, fiction, and conceptual proposals keep their chosen frame.

## Editorial character

HobFarm prose carries the author's judgment, humor, curiosity, technical understanding, peculiar connections, and appetite for the subject. Concrete objects, scenes, names, dates, mechanisms, measurements, and visual evidence supply the pressure. The writing can be blunt, funny, gothic, glamorous, grotesque, profane, cinematic, skeptical, or highly technical when the material earns it.

The reference field includes the visual intelligence and irreverence of early *MAD*, *Help!*, and *National Lampoon*, alongside the documentary detail and information design of publications such as *LIFE*, *Playboy*, and *National Geographic*. HobFarm uses that energy through its own subjects, voice, images, and systems.

## Author knowledge and evidence

The author's identifications, firsthand observations, photographs, recollections, experiments, and subject knowledge are source evidence. Research gives each material claim an appropriate evidence state and seeks corroboration in proportion to the consequence of being wrong.

A missing external receipt leaves an author identification or recollection in that state. New evidence may corroborate, qualify, complicate, or contradict it. Material changes remain visible to the author.

Never fabricate the author's memories, reactions, opinions, dialogue, identifications, experiences, motivations, chronology, or connective events. Use them only when the author supplied them or evidence supports them, and preserve the stated confidence and provenance.

Keep documented fact, recollection, author-confirmed fact or identification, inference, interpretation, opinion, open question or uncertainty, and externally unresolved claim distinct. These evidence states can inform one another, but they are not interchangeable. A recollection is not an external fact. Failed corroboration does not erase an author identification. An inference retains the facts and reasoning that support it, while interpretation and opinion remain attributable to their source.

## Source authority

The current user request controls the desired result. The repository controls its present technical state. Approved HobFarm copy and project documents carry durable editorial decisions. Author notes and selected media carry source material. ChatGPT-created outlines, plans, and task packets propose a route through that material. Research tests factual claims and expands the available evidence.

Codex reconciles those layers before implementation. A difference between a plan and the repository becomes either a current equivalent, a requested change, or an explicit unresolved decision.

## Internet-native magazine

HobFarm can use full-resolution images, archival scans, maps, source links, audio, video, animation, interactive diagrams, galleries, calculators, small applications, and connected article routes. Each medium should perform an editorial job: establish place, prove a change, explain a mechanism, reveal scale, preserve an artifact, carry a joke, or let the reader explore the subject directly.

The result should offer the depth, surprise, visual pleasure, and editorial nerve of an independent publication while using the web for things a printed magazine could never contain.
