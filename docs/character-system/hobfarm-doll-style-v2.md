# HobFarm Doll Style Profile v2

Profile ID: `HF-DOLL`

Use this profile when the user names the HobFarm doll style, supplies matching reference canon, or continues a character established in it. Use `hobfarm-visual-style-system.md` as the portable house layer.

## Face

- Eyes occupy roughly one-quarter to one-third of face height.
- Irises read as convex domes with one large highlight and one smaller secondary highlight.
- Upper lashes form clean, top-heavy graphic masses.
- Profile and three-quarter views preserve the visible eye's scale and dome shape.
- Nose is a small suggestion.
- Lips are small, full, glossy, and clearly shaped.
- Jaw is soft and rounded, with a large cranium and short chin.
- Makeup uses clean geometry, deliberate blush, and one identifier or asymmetry.

## Proportions

- Use a 5.5 to 6.5 head fashion-doll range.
- Head reads large against the frame.
- Limbs are long, joints are slim, and hands are small but complete.
- Each character receives one silhouette family and keeps it across assets.
- Adult styling comes through posture, wardrobe, expression, and proportion.

## Rendering

- Use clean dark linework with the strongest weight on the outer contour.
- Skin uses smooth airbrushed gradients.
- Hair uses grouped shapes with controlled strand detail.
- Materials keep their native finish.
- Vinyl, latex, gloss, metal, wet lips, and liquid receive crisp highlights.
- Matte cloth, lace, mesh, denim, fur, and skin retain distinct surface behavior.
- Lighting comes from a small number of readable sources.
- Detail order: silhouette, face, outfit construction, motif, surface accents.

## Liquid signature

Every character carries one deliberate liquid element. Define substance, color, placement, gravity behavior, and meaning. Liquid beads, hangs, stretches, pools, and reacts to the pose.

## Variation

Vary palette, skin tone or body material, face key, hair silhouette, species traits, wardrobe register, motif placement, liquid channel, pose, gaze, and energy.

Different characters receive distinct face keys, silhouettes, and palettes. Established characters retain theirs.

## Output targets

### Single

9:16 by default. Character fills the frame. Face and motif remain center-safe. Background stays seamless or lightly contextual. Pose shows identity and outfit construction.

### Sheet

16:9. Left quarter is a forward-facing portrait. Remaining area shows front, back, and true 45-degree three-quarter full-body views. Use one resolved design, white seamless background, flat studio light, complete hands, feet, footwear, garment backs, closures, and attachment points.

### Hero, Poster, Wallpaper

Use the active theatrical pose card, three depth planes, one emotional hook, one environment story, and center-safe face and silhouette.

## Replacement rules

- Hold one stylization level across the image or set.
- Use invented, brand-safe symbols and graphics.
- Keep strange concepts specific through exact shapes, materials, props, and body features.
- Give each character a distinct face key.
- Use clean image-only composition unless title treatment is requested.
- Replace repeated hair, bow, makeup, and liquid placements with a new head silhouette, identifier, motif location, and liquid channel.
