# POD product and content system

Status: working operating model. The Melting Rabbit Hole Dad Hat is the first test product. Nothing in this document makes a product buyable.

## The useful loop

HobFarm can use one product across commerce, production, publishing, social media, and education:

```text
design the physical item
verify the Printful product and order a sample
build a durable mockup set
apply the item to HobFarm avatars and characters
make product photos, scenes, and short videos
publish the finished work and production notes
send interested viewers to the product page
turn the method into a Workshop case study or Academy lesson
adapt the same item for the marketplace that owns that format
```

The product is the subject of the work, not a prop pasted onto unrelated content. A hat can appear on an avatar because the same design, placement, scale, and silhouette have already been tested against a real sample and a clean mockup.

## One product record, several working assets

Each approved POD product needs a private production packet:

- logical HobFarm product and variant codes;
- Printful template, store-product, catalog-product, and catalog-variant IDs;
- approved print file and placement data;
- sample photographs and approval notes;
- product cost, first-item shipping, additional-item shipping, retail price, margin, and free-shipping contribution;
- customer-facing web mockups;
- transparent or isolated product views for avatar and mannequin work;
- prompt notes, masks, scale references, and failure examples;
- source and output rights;
- related Workshop, Academy, article, gallery, character, and social records.

Public product pages receive only the approved retail information and web-sized media. Print files, masks, internal cost data, provider IDs, and full production packets stay out of public content, HTML, feeds, and metadata.

## Physical and visual approval

The physical sample is the reference. Check logo size, placement, edge quality, black level, cap shape, comfort, and how the design reads at normal viewing distance.

The reusable mockup set should include:

1. clean front, three-quarter, side, and back product views;
2. transparent or isolated views suitable for compositing;
3. one scale reference;
4. owner-shot sample photos under neutral light;
5. one approved hero image for the product page;
6. avatar and character tests that preserve the hat's crown, brim, logo placement, and material;
7. failures showing common warping, floating brims, misplaced logos, and hair collisions.

Store the public set under a durable CDN path such as:

```text
https://cdn.hob.farm/products/melting-rabbit-hole-dad-hat/
```

Keep the print file and high-resolution production assets in private storage.

## Character and mannequin branch

The avatar and mannequin work is a production method with its own checks:

1. start with an approved product view and a known character or mannequin;
2. match head angle and camera perspective;
3. fit the crown around the skull instead of placing a flat sticker over the head;
4. resolve hair, ears, horns, eyewear, and brim occlusion;
5. preserve the printed mark without redrawing it;
6. compare the result with the sample and reject impossible shapes;
7. save the prompt, mask, source images, model, settings, and correction notes.

The result can become a Gallery set, a Workshop breakdown, an Academy lesson, or product-page media. A DeviantArt mannequin can demonstrate the same method while the downloadable mannequin or character asset remains on DeviantArt.

## Video and social branch

A product-placement video needs three separate claims:

- the character or avatar is synthetic or transformed when disclosure is useful;
- the hat is a real HobFarm product;
- the link goes to the shelf that owns the transaction.

HeyGen can present the hat on an established avatar, but the video still needs a real product cutaway or sample shot. That keeps the advertisement tied to an object a buyer will actually receive.

Each finished production can yield:

- one product-page hero or supporting video;
- one short vertical product clip;
- one Workshop clip showing the compositing method;
- one social fragment built around the character;
- one social fragment built around the physical object;
- one longer YouTube or Academy explanation when the method is strong enough.

The educational claim should come from the finished work. HobFarm sells the object, shows the system that produced and promoted it, and then teaches the repeatable parts. The course is evidence-backed because the product, sample, store, media, and fulfillment path exist.

## Marketplace branches

Each platform keeps one job:

| Platform | Product-system role |
| --- | --- |
| Made by HobFarm | Direct approved Printful goods and the customer relationship |
| Etsy | Craft files, printable pieces, clip art, scrapbook material, and POD-specific derivatives suited to Etsy |
| DeviantArt | Mannequins, character assets, outfits, premium packs, and visual demonstrations |
| eBay | Counted physical inventory and one-off objects |
| Workshop | Public production evidence, tests, revisions, and failures |
| Academy | Structured lessons and reusable methods |
| YouTube and social media | Demonstration, product placement, story fragments, and traffic back to the correct page |

Do not duplicate one listing everywhere by default. Adapt the product, asset, and story to the job of the platform.

## What the system defends

The advantage is the chain of finished work:

- original HobFarm designs and characters;
- real products and samples;
- reusable mockup and compositing methods;
- publishing and gallery context;
- avatar, mannequin, and video workflows;
- direct commerce and marketplace shelves;
- support records and fulfillment experience;
- courses built from tested production.

Someone can copy a hat idea or a tutorial topic. Reproducing the connected catalog, characters, production records, media library, audience paths, and operational experience takes sustained work.

## Pilot success criteria

The hat pilot succeeds when:

- the Printful API confirms the intended store and product records;
- one exact variant is allowlisted;
- a sample passes inspection;
- standard and additional-item shipping costs are recorded;
- retail price, fixed shipping, and free-shipping threshold preserve the approved margin;
- a two-item cart and the threshold boundary pass checkout tests;
- at least one avatar or character image passes the physical-reference check;
- one product video and one Workshop explanation are published;
- the product page links to the related work and the related work links back to the product;
- an owner test order reaches delivery and reconciliation without manual data repair.
