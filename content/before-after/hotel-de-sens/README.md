# Hôtel de Sens Before & After package

This directory holds the evidence, structured metadata, captions, credits, motion prompts, and integration decisions for the Hôtel de Sens feature.

- `research.md` — verified dossier, interpretation boundaries, and open questions
- `timeline.json` — structured historical milestones
- `gallery.json` — ordered media, captions, alt text, dimensions, credits, and rights
- `sources.json` — normalized institutional source records
- `gallery-copy.md` — publication-ready gallery and social copy
- `credits.md` — compact and full credit blocks
- `video-prompts.md` — full, short, reverse, negative, continuity, and review prompts
- `integration.md` — site layout, asset paths, accessibility, and publication gate
- `article.md` — pointer to the canonical finished article draft

Web derivatives live under `public/articles/hotel-de-sens-before-and-after/`. Large institutional source files are intentionally not committed; their stable records and rights are preserved in `sources.json`.
