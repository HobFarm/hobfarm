# Expanded prompt: Zima mannequin to avatar Process Film

## Title and style block

Create two silent-first HobFarm Workshop films from one structured six-stage manifest.

- Vertical homepage/social composition: 1080 × 1920, 20.4 seconds.
- Wide Workshop composition: 1920 × 1080, 28.2 seconds.
- Background `#07060B`; surfaces `#0E0B16` and `#15101F`; borders `#2C2440`.
- Primary text `#ECE9F5`; secondary `#C8C6D4`; muted `#9B96AD`.
- Structure `#3B6FE0` and `#6B93ED`; signal `#19E3E3` and `#5CF0F0`; identity accent `#A06BFF`.
- IBM Plex Sans for headlines and explanation. IBM Plex Mono for stage numbers, metadata, locked/variable labels, and timers.
- Mood: cinematic production contact sheet, editorial process record, character-development plate, cold workshop light.

## Rhythm declaration

`establish-build-build-assemble-PEAK-resolve`

The first stage establishes the identity constraints. Stages two through four build and assemble the system. Stage five is the still-image peak. Stage six resolves the system through existing motion and a clear end claim.

## Global rules

- Keep Zima's identity visible at every stage: pale skin, gold eyes, blunt electric-blue hair, red lips, compact proportions.
- Use one dominant main plate, a persistent reference strip, and a readable text block in every scene.
- Add 2-5 structural atmosphere elements per scene: radial glow, ghost stage numeral, registration crosses, hairline grid, crop brackets, or moving coordinate ticks.
- Keep one ambient motion per scene. Share it across the decorative elements when practical.
- Use the directional plate reveal for most scene changes. Use one circular focus reveal for the hero scene and one blur-through for the avatar handoff.
- Every visual element receives an entrance. The outgoing scene remains intact until the transition covers it.
- Do not animate scene exits. The final scene may darken slightly during the last hold.
- Keep all motion deterministic and seekable. No random values, wall-clock animation, or infinite repeats.
- All video is muted and `playsinline`. Meaning lives in stage text, shot direction, locked/variable notes, and captions.

## Scene 01: Write the brief

### Concept

The film opens inside an identity-control desk. The close portrait feels like evidence under inspection, while the neutral sheet waits behind it as the first proof that a written constraint can survive a change of scale. The viewer should feel that the character existed before the costume and world.

### Mood direction

Editorial contact sheet, camera slate, cold light table, precise but not clinical.

### Depth layers

- BG: blue radial pool drifting slowly right; oversized ghost `01`; two registration crosses; faint grain.
- MG: canonical Zima portrait, neutral sheet preview, stage number, headline, explanation, shot-direction card.
- FG: identity chips, a cyan scan line, crop brackets, timing label.

### Choreography

- Ghost numeral BREATHES in from 94% scale.
- Portrait SLIDES from the left with a small perspective settle.
- Neutral sheet RISES behind it and locks to the main frame.
- Stage kicker TYPES ON through opacity and tracking.
- Headline BUILDS from the first line with a short leftward settle.
- Explanation FADES in slower than the headline.
- Shot-direction card DRAWS its top rule, then its copy appears.
- Identity chips CASCADE in under 450 ms.
- Registration marks DRIFT six to twelve pixels during the hold.

### Transition out

Directional plate reveal from bottom to top, 0.5 seconds, `power3.inOut`.

## Scene 02: Build the mannequin

### Concept

The neutral sheet becomes the whole workbench. Front, three-quarter, back, and face views remain simultaneously readable, while crop windows isolate the face and silhouette. The viewer should see consistency as an active production choice.

### Mood direction

Model-sheet review, wardrobe fitting room, measured studio calm.

### Depth layers

- BG: subtle coordinate grid; ghost `02`; narrow blue glow under the sheet; two corner ticks.
- MG: full neutral mannequin board, stage copy, shot-direction card.
- FG: face crop, proportion brackets, locked and variable rows, source portrait thumbnail.

### Choreography

- Sheet EXPANDS from a centered crop window into its final frame.
- Face crop DROPS into the reference strip.
- Proportion brackets DRAW vertically, then hold.
- Headline SLIDES in from the right while the explanation rises from below.
- Locked row CASCADES from face to stance.
- Variable row FOLLOWS from wardrobe to world.
- Grid PAN travels left during the hold.

### Transition out

Directional plate reveal from right to left, 0.48 seconds, `power3.inOut`.

## Scene 03: Define the visual language

### Concept

The neutral figure resolves into an ornate PsyGoth Blue portrait whose symbols, border, star field, ice crystals, lace, vinyl, and silver hardware each occupy a named job. The frame should feel like a design language being compiled rather than a filter being applied.

### Mood direction

Fashion material library, animation color key, technical art-direction board.

### Depth layers

- BG: violet glow with a slow pulse; ghost `03`; horizontal scan rule; registration marks.
- MG: finished astral Zima portrait, stage copy, explanation.
- FG: border and face crops, locked/variable notes, neutral source-sheet thumbnail.

### Choreography

- Ornamental border REVEALS through a horizontal mask.
- Face and jewelry crop SLIDES in beneath it.
- Full PsyGoth portrait SETTLES from a slight rightward perspective angle.
- Headline STAMPS in with firm `expo.out` timing.
- Explanation FLOATS in more slowly.
- Cyan scan line TRAVELS once across the board.
- Violet glow PULSES on the seekable timeline.

### Transition out

Directional plate reveal from left to right, 0.48 seconds, `power3.inOut`.

## Scene 04: Dress the character

### Concept

The finished portrait stays intact while the earlier wardrobe board remains visible in the source strip. The viewer can trace black lace, blue vinyl, silver chains, crystal, and smoke from inspectable parts into the denser final world. This is assembly, not morphing.

### Mood direction

Occult fashion plate, costume breakdown, controlled gothic construction.

### Depth layers

- BG: narrow cyan light column; ghost `04`; shifting corner ticks; grain.
- MG: full ornate Zima portrait, stage copy, shot direction.
- FG: earlier wardrobe-board crop, locked/variable notes, material connections.

### Choreography

- Portrait plate RISES into the central frame.
- Earlier wardrobe reference SLIDES to the far edge and holds.
- Border, jewelry, and corset details CASCADE through the crop.
- Headline PUNCHES in with a short scale settle.
- Locked and variable notes alternate left/right entrances.
- Cyan light column DRIFTS gently during the hold.

### Transition out

Circular focus reveal centered on Zima's face, 0.58 seconds, `power2.inOut`.

## Scene 05: Direct the frame

### Concept

The final still takes over the frame. Zima is centered against the ice world, while lens, depth, light, and environment notes sit at the edges like camera instructions. This is the visual payoff before motion.

### Mood direction

Cinematic key art, cold stage lighting, controlled gothic scale.

### Depth layers

- BG: full ice-world image with a restrained push; ghost `05`; aurora-colored glow.
- MG: framed hero still, headline, explanation, shot-direction card.
- FG: aspect-ratio bracket, center-line, reference board thumbnails, locked/variable rows.

### Choreography

- Hero image PUSHES forward four percent through its wrapper.
- Center-line DRAWS from top and bottom toward the face.
- Headline enters from the left with `power4.out` confidence.
- Explanation FADES in after the image has landed.
- Aspect bracket SNAPS into position.
- Reference thumbnails CASCADE along the lower edge.

### Transition out

Restrained blur-through, 0.55 seconds, incoming avatar clears from 18 px blur with `power3.out`.

## Scene 06: Turn the character into an avatar

### Concept

The approved still becomes the published HeyGen performance without redesigning Zima. One readable gesture and one line of captioned meaning close the chain. The film ends on the larger production promise rather than on a provider logo.

### Mood direction

Broadcast close, presenter test, finished Workshop evidence.

### Depth layers

- BG: blue-black field; ghost `06`; slow cyan ring drift; final registration marks.
- MG: existing Zima avatar clip, stage headline, explanation, shot direction.
- FG: caption panel, source strip, locked/variable notes, final end-message plate.

### Choreography

- Video frame CLEARS from blur and settles at full contrast.
- Headline RISES with `expo.out`.
- Explanation and shot card enter from opposing sides.
- Caption panel DRAWS on, then its text appears.
- End message BUILDS near the final hold.
- Final registration marks DRIFT outward by eight pixels.

### Transition out

Final scene only: a dark overlay rises to 30% during the last 0.7 seconds while the end message remains readable.

## Recurring motifs

- The active stage number appears as a large ghost numeral and a small mono label.
- Cyan registration crosses mark the working frame.
- The source strip stays along the lower or side edge and always contains the last approved reference.
- Locked and variable lists use the same positions across both formats.
- A single traveling hairline connects the brief to the final avatar stage.

## Negative prompt

- No HeyGen branding, pastel HeyGen layout, copied typography, or provider marks.
- No generic dashboard cards, gradient text, glassmorphism, or full-screen linear gradients.
- No unrelated character, mascot, costume, skin color, hair color, or world.
- No infinite loops, jump cuts, random movement, or wall-clock animation.
- No speech-only meaning, tiny labels, cropped source thumbnails, or hidden transcript content.
