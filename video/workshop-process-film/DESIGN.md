# HobFarm Workshop Process Film design

## Intent

The film should look like a production record in motion: part contact sheet, part camera slate, part character-development board. It uses the current HobFarm Workshop shell and keeps Zima's blue PsyGoth identity as the focal color event. The finished reference is an ornate astral portrait with geometric diagrams, ice crystals, silver smoke, black lace, and polished blue vinyl; its density is the payoff after the neutral sheet.

## Typography

- Display and body: `IBM Plex Sans`, using 300/350 for explanation and 650/700 for stage headlines.
- Data and labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, using tabular numerals and uppercase tracking.
- Vertical: 76-92 px headlines, 28-31 px explanation, 18-22 px labels.
- Wide: 72-90 px headlines, 28-34 px explanation, 18-22 px labels.
- Keep display tracking near `-0.04em`; mono labels use `0.12em` to `0.18em`.

## Shape, spacing, and depth

- Use square or 12-18 px corners. The frames should read as production plates, not soft product cards.
- Use 2 px structural borders and visible cyan registration marks.
- Use radial glows, oversized ghost numerals, hairline rules, and restrained grain for depth.
- Keep one large media plate and one clear text block as the two primary focal points.
- Reference thumbnails remain visible at the edge of every scene.

## Motion

- Rhythm: `establish-build-build-assemble-PEAK-resolve`.
- Primary transition: directional plate reveal, 0.45-0.55 seconds, `power3.inOut`.
- Accent transition: circular focus reveal into the directed frame.
- Final transition: restrained blur-through into the avatar stage.
- Every scene uses staggered entrances with mixed directions and at least three easing signatures.
- Ambient motion stays on structural details: drifting coordinate marks, breathing glows, traveling hairlines. Zima's images receive a slow crop or parallax move through a wrapper. The new portrait is shown intact enough to read its ornamental border and symbolic field.
- No infinite repeats. All movement is deterministic and registered on the composition timeline.

## Avoid

- No HeyGen branding or copied pastel reference styling.
- No full-screen linear gradients, generic glass cards, gradient text, or soft SaaS UI.
- No new colors, logos, mascots, or unrelated character images.
- No jump cuts, camera acrobatics, outfit morphing, or identity changes.
- No text smaller than 18 px in the rendered film.
