The Grimoire is HobFarm's visual vocabulary knowledge graph. It powers StyleFusion's prompt compilation, HobBot's content pipeline, and this knowledgebase.

## What is the Grimoire?

The Grimoire is a structured knowledge system that organizes visual vocabulary into atoms, arrangements, and correspondences. Every creative concept, from lighting styles to color palettes to composition techniques, lives as a classified atom in the graph.

## How it works

When StyleFusion compiles a prompt, it queries the Grimoire for relevant atoms. The conductor assembles these atoms into a coherent creative direction based on the selected arrangement. Each generation teaches the system something new: what worked, what the user kept, and what got regenerated.
