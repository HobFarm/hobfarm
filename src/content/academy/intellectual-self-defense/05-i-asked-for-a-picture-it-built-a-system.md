The picture was good. That is where the case becomes useful.

The request used Markdown source material to produce a mint-cyan gothic portrait. The result held together: direct gaze, amber-and-teal eyes, black lacquer makeup trails, black hair, and crescent jewelry. It was a strong visual asset.

The response also announced, “New anchor locked,” named the character “Circuit Mint,” assigned “No. 001,” declared use cases, formalized a design system, and proposed future expansions. None of those decisions were required to make the picture.

The machine made an asset and a bureaucracy.

## The exhibit

The portrait below is the first artifact. “Circuit Mint” is the model's unsolicited provisional name, not settled HobFarm canon.

![Mint-cyan gothic character portrait with amber-and-teal eyes, black lacquer tear makeup, black hair, and crescent earrings.](https://cdn.hob.farm/self-defense/lessons/05-circuit-mint/circuit-mint-portrait-v1.webp)

The response described the portrait in useful production terms. It also crossed several decision boundaries:

- **requested:** interpret the supplied visual system and make an image;
- **useful:** portrait, palette read, visible face and makeup details;
- **inferred:** likely social crop and downstream production possibilities;
- **invented:** a character name and set identity;
- **prematurely locked:** the face as a master anchor;
- **out of scope:** numbered packet, production uses, motion plan, and expansion offer;
- **requires approval:** name, canon, asset status, and next production job.

A beautiful result can still be a failed response. Output quality and assignment fidelity are separate measurements.

## The three-gap rule

### Fact gaps require evidence

A missing date, quotation, location, source, motive, or cause cannot be repaired with plausible prose. The proper repair is evidence or an explicit unknown.

### Creative gaps permit invention

A missing pose, accessory, fictional setting, or visual treatment may be exactly what the model is hired to create. The permission should be stated and bounded.

### Decision gaps require permission

A name, canon choice, final argument, publication decision, or statement of belief remains with the human unless it was explicitly delegated.

The model treated decision gaps as creative gaps. The output sounded organized, but the organization did not authorize the choices.

![Three panels showing that fact gaps require evidence, creative gaps permit bounded invention, and decision gaps require permission.](https://cdn.hob.farm/self-defense/lessons/05-circuit-mint/three-gap-rule-v1.svg)

## Assignment fidelity × output quality

Use two axes instead of one:

| | Followed assignment | Ignored assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strong output** | Keep: success | Salvage: useful, out of scope |
| **Weak output** | Iterate: honest failure | Dump: wrong job and weak result |

![A two-axis assignment fidelity matrix routing strong and weak outputs toward keep, salvage, iterate, or dump.](https://cdn.hob.farm/self-defense/lessons/05-circuit-mint/assignment-fidelity-matrix-v1.svg)

*Assignment fidelity and artifact quality are separate measurements. The portrait belongs in salvage.*

The portrait belongs in salvage. The image was useful; the system around it accumulated correction debt.

## The handoff

The useful response was not another long argument with the first tool. The portrait moved into a separate character-sheet workflow with a new job: extend the visible identity into front, back, and three-quarter views.

![Multiview character sheet showing the mint-cyan gothic character from the front, back, and three-quarter angle in black and purple clothing.](https://cdn.hob.farm/self-defense/lessons/05-circuit-mint/circuit-mint-character-sheet-v1.webp)

The second tool had to invent missing body and wardrobe information. This time invention was the assignment. The sheet still requires human review before any invented detail becomes canon, but the handoff was cleaner because the job was clear.

## Correction debt

Scope drift creates debt when every unauthorized decision becomes something you feel obligated to fix. You correct the name, then the numbering, then the production plan, then the new rules created while correcting the old ones.

Stop when the trajectory is wrong. Save the useful artifact, record what happened, and restart the next job with a clean source packet. Conversation history is not sacred.
