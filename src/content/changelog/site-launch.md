## hob.farm goes live

The HobFarm website launched on Cloudflare Pages. The site includes the portfolio, project showcases for StyleFusion and Grimoire, a public gallery of AI-generated artwork, articles, and the help center.

Built with Astro, styled with Tailwind CSS and DaisyUI, deployed to Cloudflare's edge network.

### What shipped

- Full portfolio site with project case studies
- Public gallery showcasing StyleFusion outputs across multiple art styles
- Articles with technical and creative content
- Help center and documentation framework
- Contact page and status monitoring
- RSS feed for article content
- Legal pages (terms, privacy, cookies, acceptable use, bug bounty)
