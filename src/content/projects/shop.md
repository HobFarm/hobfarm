The Shop is a directory, not a universal cart. Each platform has one clear job.

## Made by HobFarm

Direct HobFarm merchandise will use a small, approved Printful release. No direct product is public or buyable yet. Samples, variants, prices, checkout, and fulfillment need approval first.

## Etsy

Etsy holds craft files, clip art, scrapbook material, printables, seasonal sets, and archive packs.

## DeviantArt

DeviantArt holds mannequins, character sheets, outfits, wallpapers, premium packs, adoptables, and exclusives.

## eBay

eBay is reserved for counted physical inventory: DVDs, books, magazines, antiques, collectibles, and one-off objects. A public store link will be added when real inventory exists.

## Academy

Academy is the catalog for practical free and affordable one-time courses.

## Ko-fi

Ko-fi accepts one-time reader support. It is not a second course or marketplace catalog.

Customer problems belong in [Customer Help](/helpcenter/). Reader funding belongs on [Support HobFarm](/support/).
