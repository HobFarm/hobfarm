The HobFarm shop is where the visual experiments become products.

A design may start as a strange little idea: a haunted craft shop, a portal in a desk mat, a corrupted Halloween creature, a fictional warning label, a ghost village, a weird clay object, or a vintage find that looks like it wandered in from the wrong roadside shop. If the idea has a product path, it becomes a listing. You can see where these designs come from in the [visuals](/gallery).

The current shop system is built around repeatable product families: surreal desk mats, digital sticker and clip-art packs, drinkware, laptop sleeves, bags, soft goods, handmade polymer clay pieces, wall art, and odd vintage finds. The goal is not random merch. The goal is to build small product worlds that can become multiple useful things.

Etsy is the main storefront today. Ko-fi handles support and small drops. An on-site shop and eBay are later shelves, not open yet.

## Surreal Desk Mats

Wide-format desk worlds, portal surfaces, cracked-wall illusions, 3D breach effects, haunted villages, psychedelic office landscapes, and strange scenes built for gamers, workstations, artists, and people who do not want their desk to look like a corporate supply catalog.

## Digital Sticker & Clip-Art Packs

Low-ticket digital packs built around themes: Halloween monsters, haunted objects, fictional warning labels, weird office decals, ravens, pumpkins, ghosts, portal fragments, and other reusable graphic sets. These are downloadable assets for printable stickers, digital journals, collage, crafting, and personal projects.

## Drinkware

Mugs and tumblers, pint glasses, and related drinkware using simplified graphics, wrap concepts, haunted labels, weird souvenir-shop marks, and product-friendly motifs pulled from larger HobFarm worlds.

## Bags & Laptop Sleeves

Totes, laptop sleeves, duffle bags, and other carry goods using bold central graphics, pattern crops, fictional brand marks, portal emblems, and design fragments from larger collections.

## Handmade Clay Oddities

Small handmade polymer clay pieces, magnets, charms, ghost blobs, portal drips, haunted signposts, miniature relics, ornaments, and tactile experiments that give the shop a handmade layer beyond print-on-demand.

## Vintage Finds

Odd, useful, nostalgic, decorative, and collectible pieces found through local hunting, estate sales, auctions, and weird little treasure paths. These are one-off shelf items in the broader HobFarm gift-shop system.

## Corrupted Craft Village

A product world built from rural souvenir-house nostalgia, antique shops, craft stores, New England roads, ghost villages, haunted windows, folk architecture, ooze, portals, trippy liquid, and supernatural gift-shop logic. Call it corrupted cottagecore if you need a label, but the tone stays dark, strange, and product-focused.

This is not a replica of any existing collectible-house product. It is a translation of that emotional category into HobFarm formats: desk mats, mugs and tumblers, posters, sticker packs, digital art, and later handmade clay add-ons.

## Product Media Pipeline

Finished products do not stop at the listing image.

HobFarm also builds sales media around each product: mockups, lifestyle images, avatar product-placement videos, short social clips, website shop features, and Etsy listing assets. A cup can become a product photo, a lifestyle image, a video with a presenter, a short social ad, and a shop-page feature.
