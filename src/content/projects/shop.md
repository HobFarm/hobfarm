The Shop is a directory, not a universal cart. Each platform has one clear job.

## Etsy

Etsy is the home for reusable themed files, clip art, backgrounds, textures, printable elements, scrapbook material, seasonal sets, and rebuilt archive packs. The archive is being rebuilt, so the HobFarm page does not promise live inventory.

## DeviantArt

DeviantArt is the lane for mannequin bases, character sheets, outfits, scenes, world packs, wallpapers, adoptables, exclusives, and collector downloads.

## eBay

eBay is reserved for counted physical inventory: books, media, collectibles, equipment, antiques, and other objects sold one at a time. [Browse HobFarm on eBay](https://www.ebay.com/usr/hobfarm).
