The raccoon is the first entry in **Cute and Corrupted Critters**. It starts as a wholesome storybook animal: a chibi raccoon sitting in a sunlit clover glade, holding a four-leaf clover, surrounded by toadstools, dewdrops, moss, and a tiny carved house sign. The corrupted version keeps the same critter, the same seated pose, and the same clover, then rebuilds the world around it into a glowing toxic-waste site.

## Video Preview

The short video uses a simple two-image structure: cute raccoon first, toxic raccoon second. The motion comes from editing, timing, and the cut rather than an expensive animation pass. It is built as a repeatable format for social posts, gallery previews, and premium packs.

## Cute Version

The cute version is soft and storybook: pastel forest greens, warm daylight, big sparkly eyes, a gentle smile, dewdrops, spotted toadstools, and a small wooden house sign. The raccoon's bandit mask and ringed tail anchor the design, and the four-leaf clover sets the lucky-glade mood.

## Corrupted Version

The corrupted version keeps the raccoon recognizable while changing the material language. Pastel softness turns into radioactive acid green, slime drips from every leaf, the eyes glow and the smile sharpens into a toothy grin, and the friendly props become hazard signage, a skull "TOXIC KEEP OUT" sign, and leaking ACID barrels.

## Transformation Logic

Cute and Corrupted Critters is not a costume swap. The goal is to keep the same-creature read while changing the surface, palette, mood, and environment. The mask, ringed tail, seated pose, and four-leaf clover stay locked; the color, lighting, texture, expression, and world around the critter all change.
