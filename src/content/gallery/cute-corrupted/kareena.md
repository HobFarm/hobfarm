Kareena started as a bright, fashion-forward character sheet built around soft pinks, navy hair, purple highlights, aqua accents, heart clips, plush accessories, and cute daily props. The corrupted version keeps the same face, hair shape, eye style, and core accessories, then pushes her into a torn zine-punk direction with black textures, fishnet, safety pins, distressed fabric, and dark club energy.

## Video Preview

This 6-second video uses a simple two-image structure: cute Kareena first, corrupted Kareena second. The motion comes from editing, timing, transition, and music instead of expensive AI animation. It is built as a repeatable format for social posts, gallery previews, and premium full-resolution sheet packs.

## Cute Version

The cute version uses a soft fashion-magazine layout with blush pink, cream, navy, lavender, aqua, and heart-shaped accessories. Her silhouette is light and playful: off-shoulder pink top, ruffle shorts, pink heels, plush charm, berry drink, and a bedroom-style mood panel. The design is bright, readable, and built around sweet accessory logic.

## Corrupted Version

The corrupted version keeps Kareena recognizable while changing the material language. Pink softness turns into black leather, torn fabric, fishnet, chains, distressed patches, punk graphics, and photocopied zine texture. The palette shifts toward charcoal, ash gray, off-white, deep purple, indigo, and teal accents. She is still cute, but the presentation is rougher, louder, and more unstable.

## Transformation Logic

The cute/corrupted format is not a random costume swap. The goal is to keep the same-person read while changing the surface language. Kareena's cute version is soft, polished, pastel, and accessory-heavy. Her corrupted version keeps the same core identity, then replaces sweetness with torn edges, black textures, punk typography, clubwear, and chaotic collage graphics.
