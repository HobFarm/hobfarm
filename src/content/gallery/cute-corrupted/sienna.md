Sienna started as a way to push the red character theme somewhere other than the obvious fire, vampire, or goth direction. Instead of building her around flames or blood, the character developed from a landscape image of Tassili n'Ajjer: sandstone formations, red earth, dust, open desert, carved rock shapes, and that strange feeling of a place that looks ancient before anyone even enters the frame.

That image shifted the design from "red fashion character" into something more atmospheric. Sienna became a plateau encounter character, the kind of figure a traveler might find at dusk while crossing a lonely stretch of rock and sand. She is not a standard desert trope, princess, warrior, witch, or vampire. She is closer to a local apparition, a cute form worn by the landscape itself.

The cute version of Sienna was built around softness and invitation. Her design uses warm dark skin with sienna undertones, large luminous aqua eyes, and oversized two-tone hair with a deep brown base and copper-terra-cotta highlights. Her outfit uses draped fabric, wrapped forms, mineral jewelry, sandstone colors, turquoise accents, and small charm-like details.

Her visual effects are part of the character system. Instead of fire, Sienna uses red dust, colored incense smoke, steam, and subtle static electricity. The dust connects her to the landscape. The incense and steam make her feel ritualistic without turning her into a cliche. The static gives her an unstable supernatural edge, like the air around her is charged before a storm.

The corrupted version keeps the same face, body shape, hair silhouette, and red-earth identity, but changes the emotional temperature. Her eyes shift to purple. Her outfit becomes darker, more seductive, and more tattered. The clean dust becomes heavier grit. The soft smoke becomes blackened incense and storm haze. The static electricity becomes brighter and more dangerous. She is still cute, but the lure has sharpened.

At the center of the design is a simple idea: Sienna is not just a character standing in a desert. She is the desert encounter.
