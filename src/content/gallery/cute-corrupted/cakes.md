Celebration Cake is the first entry in **Cute and Corrupted Cakes**. It starts as a polished bakery display: a two-tier pastel celebration cake with butter-yellow and mint tiers, lavender ruffles, pink and yellow bows, cherries, and star sprinkles, staged on a white pedestal in a bright, airy bakery. The corrupted version keeps the same cake and the same staging, then rebuilds the world around it into a candlelit gothic centerpiece.

## Cute Version

The cute version treats the cake like a professional product photo: clear tiers, clean frosting, strong pastel color design, themed bows, beautiful piping, and a gold-framed "Celebration Cake" sign marked 100% Cute.

## Corrupted Version

The corrupted version keeps the cake recognizable while flipping the mood into a darker bakery fantasy. Pastel frosting becomes black icing, ruffles turn deep purple, sprinkles become garnet jewels and gold filigree, the sweet bow topper becomes a haunted cat-face bow, and the bright bakery becomes a candlelit witch tavern. The display sign now reads "Corrupted Celebration Cake," 100% Deliciously Unhinged.

## Transformation Logic

Cute and Corrupted Cakes is not a different dessert. The goal is to keep the same-cake read while changing the frosting, palette, lighting, and setting. The tiers, bows, cherries, swags, and pedestal stay locked; the color, texture, jewels, topper, and room all change.
