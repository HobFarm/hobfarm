This gallery collects character-fashion experiments where wardrobe, setting, color, pose, and surface detail are treated like a visual grammar. Each image starts from a different world: alley punk, neon plaza, coastal goth, painted summer, occult bathroom, stage pin-up. The point is not one fixed character. The point is seeing how style behaves when it is pushed into different bodies, scenes, and moods.
