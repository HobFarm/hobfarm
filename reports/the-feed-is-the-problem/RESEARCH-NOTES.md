# The Feed Is the Problem: research notes

Research checked August 14, 2026. The article's central object is the ranked public feed as a cultural and economic system. Its claim is narrower than a general argument that social media causes mental illness: ambiguous behavioral signals can become distribution instructions, and those instructions can return to creators as production lessons.

## Evidence chain

1. The Phys.org story that arrived through Thunderbird summarizes two studies connecting workplace doomscrolling with later rumination and lower work engagement.
2. Meta's 2015 dwell-time announcement supplies a historical example of a platform converting comparatively longer viewing into a relevance signal while acknowledging that identical dwell time can have different explanations.
3. Randomized Upworthy headline tests show a scoped causal advantage for negative headline wording.
4. Mood and browsing experiments support a bidirectional relationship between mood and the emotional valence of selected web material.
5. Reddit research adds a human input: pressure to make a distinct contribution can shift a discussion toward negativity even without an instruction to be negative.
6. Creator interviews show that opaque ranking, analytics, revenue, and scheduling pressure affect later production decisions.
7. YouTube's official payment total establishes economic scale but not equal or typical creator income.
8. Arizona State University's Content Creation BA shows that production, audience strategy, analytics, ethics, law, and brand work have become formal curriculum.
9. YouTube's official August 2026 policy announcement and Help page establish a February 1, 2027 rolling threshold of 10 million qualified Shorts views for Shorts advertising and subscription revenue, continued YPP membership and long-form earnings below that threshold, and a separate 20-million-view entry path for new channels.
10. Cartoon Brew and Jeff Bruno provide the independent-animation context. The platform rule creates a production incentive; it does not prove that every creator will increase volume or adopt negative hooks.
11. The federal MDL record and August 17 reporting establish the first four state plaintiffs, broader 29-state posture, jury-selection status, alleged youth-engagement and child-data mechanisms, requested product changes, and Meta's denial.
12. Meta's 2025 Form 10-K and second-quarter 2026 Form 10-Q establish the advertising business model, 27 percent year-over-year quarterly advertising-revenue growth, and 14 percent ad-impression growth.
13. The Global Flourishing Study analysis associates satisfying adult relationships with better self-rated health but is cross-sectional and cannot establish cause.

## Causal boundaries

- Do not claim that social feeds caused increased therapy use, explain every case of distress, or make treatment unnecessary.
- Do not convert short-term mood measures into diagnoses.
- Do not generalize an Upworthy headline result to all content or audiences.
- Do not generalize Reddit's first-50-comment analysis to every discussion system.
- Do not describe the February 2027 YouTube rule as active in August 2026 or confuse Shorts revenue eligibility with YPP membership.
- Keep every Meta youth-design and child-data claim attributed to the states, include Meta's denial, and do not generalize the youth allegations to all users or platforms.
- Do not convert engagement into a fixed revenue-per-minute claim. Meta's filings document a business relationship among engagement, ad impressions, targeting, and revenue.
- Do not treat friendship as a cure or substitute for medical care.
- Keep the complete attention-to-production loop labeled as synthesis because no single paper tests the whole system.

## Editorial mesh decision

Primary section: **Culture**. Technology is the mechanism, but the article's promise concerns media habits, labor, institutions, public discussion, treatment claims, and relationships. It has no Magazine Time Machine or 3DM series membership.
