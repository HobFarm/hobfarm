# California racing source audit

Audit date: July 25, 2026

The finished article uses 27 linked sources. Institutional, museum, government,
preservation, and original-organization pages carry the main chronology. The
article narrows claims where a source establishes a broad location or district
but not an exact scene, parcel, or production count.

## Claims narrowed or omitted

- Film stills were omitted. The page compares production histories instead of
  assigning unverified tracks to individual shots.
- The Solar midget is presented as a photographed museum object. Numerical
  production and kit-sales claims were removed.
- The museum lowrider biography and owner identity were not reproduced.
- Current-use labels describe former sites or surrounding districts, not exact
  parcel boundaries.
- Salton Sea history and dead malls remain short future-article seeds.
