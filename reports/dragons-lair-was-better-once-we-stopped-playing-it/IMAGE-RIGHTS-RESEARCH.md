# Dragon’s Lair article: image and rights research

Checked August 15, 2026.

The Strong, Smithsonian, D23, arcade databases, publisher pages, and current commercial releases contain useful images of the cabinet, animation, discs, and game screens. Those pages do not supply a sufficiently clear reuse basis for HobFarm to rehost the images in this article. They remain linked research sources only.

The article therefore uses no animation frames, game screenshots, cabinet marquees, logos, advertisements, press photographs, YouTube thumbnails, or museum object images. It also does not embed a third-party full playthrough. The ending describes the author’s viewing experience without selecting one unofficial upload as the canonical version.

The visual package consists of an original HobFarm SVG and live HTML/CSS figures. The central cabinet is generic: it carries no Dragon’s Lair title, character, scene, logo, or copied cabinet art. The screen contains only an abstract directional prompt. Surrounding cabinets use abstract driving and fighting symbols.

The SVG source remains editable at `assets/dragons-lair-was-better-once-we-stopped-playing-it/hero.svg`. Published derivatives are immutable new R2 objects recorded in `asset-manifest.json`.
