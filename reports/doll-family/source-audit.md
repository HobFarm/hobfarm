# Doll Family source audit

## Editorial decisions

- The article treats Gracie, Harry, Daisy, and Tiny as working performers and family members. Daisy’s 1931 portrait remains the discovery artifact, not the subject by herself.
- Claims about the siblings’ migration order, the Schneider family’s size, Bert Earles’s legal role, and the first American wild-west employer were narrowed because the reviewed sources disagree.
- The Buffalo Bill poster illustrates period touring publicity. It is explicitly not offered as evidence that the family worked for William F. Cody.
- AFI supports Harry’s identified Lollipop Guild placement in *The Wizard of Oz*. The reviewed evidence did not support precise Munchkin placements for Gracie, Daisy, or Tiny, so the article leaves them unresolved.
- The current Laurel Park Airbnb calls its Doll Family link a rumor. The article preserves that qualification and distinguishes the rental from the documented Bee Ridge Road property lead.
- Modern casting arguments are attributed to the performers and organizations making them. The article does not treat one actor as a spokesman for everyone with dwarfism.

## Image audit

- The image shows Harry Earles with Olga Baclanova. The corrected filename and article caption now agree.
- The supplied `freaks-1932-original-one-sheet.jpg` is used as original-release advertising. `film-poster-freaks.jpg` is identified as later Dwain Esper / Excelsior reissue advertising.
- Three Florida Memory images were retained with record identifiers, creator credits, source links, and Public Domain Mark 1.0 rights notes.
- The National Museum of American History’s Doll Family group image was not copied. The object page marks the image with usage conditions; the metadata’s CC0 status does not establish unrestricted reuse of the photograph.
- Low-resolution, unattributed, duplicate, or visually ambiguous image leads were rejected.
