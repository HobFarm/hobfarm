## Current passages narrowed during integration

- Rumble remains one dated homepage visit, not a claim about the whole service or all users.
- The Psycho social post is analyzed as a compressed factoid. The article makes no claim about whether a human or model wrote it.
- The AI section uses provider policy environment and distinguishes models, hosted apps, APIs, regions, classifiers, and account layers.
- Moderation and private platform decisions are not described as legally identical to government censorship.

## Claims excluded

The finished copy does not claim that Arbuckle caused the Code, Psycho killed it, Europe had no censors, ratings created New Hollywood, Star Wars killed New Hollywood, War of the Worlds caused nationwide mass panic, PMRC labels always increased sales, Reddit copied YouTube, Rumble is entirely right-wing, social factoids are necessarily AI-written, or that one national policy binary explains model behavior.
