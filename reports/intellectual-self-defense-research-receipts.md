# Intellectual Self-Defense research receipts

Checked for the HobFarm article and Academy course on July 21, 2026.

## Phrase lineage

- [Noam Chomsky, “Intellectuals and the Responsibilities of Public Life”](https://chomsky.info/20010527/) is the primary source for the phrase lineage used in the article. Chomsky describes encouraging readers to undertake “a course in intellectual self-defense” and explicitly identifies Edward S. Herman as his co-author of *Manufacturing Consent*. The HobFarm course title is presented as a synthesis, not as a six-word quotation from Chomsky.
- [Seven Stories Press, *A Short Course in Intellectual Self-Defense*](https://www.sevenstories.com/books/2843-a-short-course-in-intellectual-self-defense) confirms the English publication record for Normand Baillargeon's 2008 book and its broader coverage of logic, language, numbers, science, politics, and media. The HobFarm course is described as a narrower workshop on AI research, production, scope, and mixed-origin media.

## Research and media claims

- [Library of Congress, “How to Use the Card Catalog”](https://guides.loc.gov/card-catalog/using-the-card-catalog) supports the historical description of author, title, and subject cards and the catalog as the Library's primary discovery system before its online catalog.
- [NIST AI 600-1, *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) supports the course's treatment of confabulation, unreliable citations, information integrity, human oversight, and source verification. The publication record was checked against the official NIST DOI landing page.
- [C2PA, “Content Credentials: C2PA Technical Specification Explainer”](https://c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/explainer/Explainer.html) supports the distinction between provenance and truth. The article keeps that limit explicit: a documented origin or edit history does not establish factual accuracy.
- [Wineburg et al., “Lateral Reading on the Open Internet”](https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000740) is the primary study behind the course's recommendation to leave an unfamiliar page and investigate the source through the open web.

## Claims kept qualified

- Chatbot behavior is described by job and observed workflow, not as a timeless claim about every product.
- Markdown source files are presented as inspectable steering records, not as guarantees of originality, accuracy, or fidelity.
- Historical catalogs and modern generative systems are compared through visible and compressed handoffs; they are not described as identical machinery.
- Provenance helps establish origin and history. It does not settle truth.
