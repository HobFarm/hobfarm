# Deserts Remember Water captions and rights

Agency files were acquired and processed on August 13, 2026. Their originals are preserved under `assets/articles/deserts-remember-water/originals/`, with WebP derivatives under `public/articles/deserts-remember-water/`. The HobFarm photograph package was recovered from R2 on August 14. Those originals remain under `hobfarm-cdn/articles/desert-water/`; the article requests responsive Cloudflare Image Resizing variants and does not overwrite them.

## Derived files

- `hero.webp`: 1600 by 900 editorial crop of the USGS Lake Cahuilla photograph. Retained but no longer referenced by the article.
- `social.webp`: 1200 by 630 editorial crop of the same photograph. Retained but no longer referenced by the article.
- `*-xl.webp`: maximum 2400 pixels wide without source upscaling.
- `*-lg.webp`: maximum 1600 pixels wide without source upscaling.
- `*-md.webp`: maximum 1000 pixels wide without source upscaling.
- `*-thumb.webp`: maximum 480 pixels wide.

## Supplied files held back

- `lake-mead-las-vegas-bay-1950s.jpg` through `lake-mead-las-vegas-bay-1950s3.jpg` and `nevada-lake-mead-2020-vs-1950s.jpg`: item-level source and reuse records were not attached. The article does not publish them.
- `Nevada - Lake Mead Near Hoover Dam.jpg`: redundant with the sourced USGS bathtub-ring image.
- `nevada-hoover-dam-arizona-spillway-tunnel.jpg`: not needed for the article's water-memory argument.
- `nevada-lake-mead-shoreline-trail.jpg`: useful field image, but redundant after the two larger Lake Mead panoramas.

The NPS Tule Springs megafauna reconstruction also remains excluded. Its page credits artist Julius Csotonyi but does not state a clear public reuse license.
