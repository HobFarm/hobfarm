## Research findings

| Subject | Primary source and version | Claims cleared for publication | Boundary kept in the article |

| Amazon ebook output and usage | Reimers and Waldfogel, [NBER Working Paper 34777](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34777), revised July 2026 | Stratified sample over 330,000 titles; nearly 50,000-title detection sample; monthly releases nearly tripled after 2022; detected AI above half of 2025 releases; average usage declined; modest-use count rose; author selection explains most of the usage gap; model estimates roughly 7 percent more consumer surplus in 2025. | Amazon ebooks are not all books.
