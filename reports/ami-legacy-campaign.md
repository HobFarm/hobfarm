# Ami / Legacy: The Electric Future Wagon

## Campaign premise

Legacy begins with real carriage drawings and asks what their engineering logic becomes when the horse disappears.

- Two-wheel forms keep the pull architecture. A robot replaces the horse and can vary from compact quadruped to more ceremonial mechanical animal.
- Four-wheel forms become battery-electric and autonomous. They carry their own propulsion, navigation, and safety systems.
- The silhouette stays recognizable. The power system, control system, lighting, materials, and use case move forward.

Ami is the spokesperson because she can sell the fantasy straight. The image can read as a luxury vehicle campaign while the copy leaves room for a fake-commercial cut on HobFarm TV.
