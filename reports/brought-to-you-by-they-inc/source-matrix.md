## Matrix reading rule

The establishment and rival columns describe recognizable product forms. They do not certify that either package is false. Equal inspection does not require equal blame, equal factual performance, equal reach, or equal harm. The article’s claim is about the transformation from event to portable social conclusion. Evidence still decides individual claims.
