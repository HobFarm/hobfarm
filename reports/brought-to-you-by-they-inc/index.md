## Editorial scope

The article is a sourced personal overview, not a complete theory or representative history of every media system. It uses equal inspection rather than equal blame: every faction's claims face the same source and scope tests, while documented differences in truth, power, reach, and harm remain visible.

Its factual architecture separates documented facts, historical interpretations, narrator recollections, and the They, Inc. satire. Mechanism-level research now supports the article's claims about audience demand, repetition, social incentives, political identity, and the limits of the metaphor.

## Rights and provenance

The hero is original HobFarm editorial art generated for this article, then typeset and finished locally. The initial source gave the presenter an extra arm. The corrected v2 source has exactly two arms and two hands; it was published under a new immutable key and is the only hero version referenced by the article. Supporting diagrams were created locally from the article’s argument. The Iran–Djibouti graphic incorporates cropped Google News screenshots under criticism and commentary, alongside an original schematic map.

No third-party photograph is published. The Lady Gaga street-image specimen named in the brief was not supplied with the attachments, and its origin remains unresolved. It was cut instead of being reconstructed or substituted.
