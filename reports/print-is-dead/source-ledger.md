## Editorial boundary

This is a sourced route through one comedy cluster, not a definitive social graph of American comedy. A finished credit is treated differently from an unrealized casting idea, a participant's later recollection, and an institutional-alumni route. The article keeps those edge types visible instead of flattening them into a single claim that everybody worked together.

The article belongs to Film & TV. It does not qualify for Magazine Time Machine because no particular magazine artifact originated the investigation. It does not qualify for 3 Degrees of Dick Miller because the route has no material Dick Miller connection.
