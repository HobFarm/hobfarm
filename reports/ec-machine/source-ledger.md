## Accident bibliography note

The newspaper reconstruction identifies two contemporary _New York Times_ items: “Two Men Are Killed in Crash of Motorboats on Lake Placid” (August 21, 1947) and “Blamed in Boat Crash: Mrs. Cohen Is Called Negligent by Lake Placid Coroner” (August 30, 1947, p. 17). Those articles were not directly retrievable during this reporting pass. They remain bibliographic leads rather than silently presented primary-source reads.

## Editorial decisions

- The article does not assign a single inventor to the comic book. It describes a sequence of printing, advertising, syndication, distribution, and publishing experiments.
- Max Gaines is credited as a publisher and format builder, not as the creator of Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, or the DC universe.
- The Senate hearings, private pressure campaigns, distributor power, and the Comics Code are kept distinct.
- The move of _Mad_ to magazine format has two stated consequences: Gaines’s recollected attempt to retain Kurtzman and practical escape from comic-book Code jurisdiction.
