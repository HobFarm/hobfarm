## Selection notes

- No museum, auction, marketplace, blog, or scanned-comic image was copied into the article. Those candidates carried unnecessary ownership or reuse uncertainty.
- The two film frames are necessary evidence for the article’s opening observation. They are kept subordinate to the criticism, captioned as frame excerpts, and not offered as downloads.
- Alt text identifies what a reader needs for the argument. Captions identify the work and the commentary context without making a rights claim.
- The source SVGs remain in the reporting folder so future corrections can be made without reconstructing the art.
