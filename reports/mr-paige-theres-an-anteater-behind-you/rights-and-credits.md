## Dalí archive

The local archive and the packet’s selected scans were used as research inputs only. The official Fundació Gala-Salvador Dalí catalogue states that reproduction rights are controlled by the Spanish State through the Foundation and directs licensing through VEGAP. No reproduction license was supplied or purchased for this task.

For that reason:

- no Dalí scan was copied into `public/`;
- no Foundation image was hotlinked;
- the article uses text-only catalogue cards linking to official records;
- `artwork-index.csv` records all 323 local JPG files without modifying the archive;
- unidentified or incompletely identified files remain excluded.
