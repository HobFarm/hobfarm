# Hit the Source Directly: graphics plan and placement

The visual package contains five original HobFarm vector graphics and one author-supplied ChatGPT illustration. The four technical body diagrams are portrait-oriented so their labels remain legible when rendered at 322 pixels wide in a 390-pixel mobile viewport. The supplied landscape illustration appears at full width with an expandable transcript, while a dedicated crop supplies social metadata.

| Graphic | Job | Placement | Accessibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct line | Show HobFarm releases crossing an open feed line above the engagement factory | Hero | Frontmatter alt text and opening caption |
| Engagement factory illustration | Contrast a direct publisher-to-reader pipeline with machinery driven by outrage, reaction, repetition, and retention | Lead body figure after the central claim; dedicated 1200 by 630 social crop | Alt text, caption, generation credit, full-size link, expandable transcript |
| Two pipelines | Separate engagement ranking from reader-chosen source delivery and a personal downstream filter | Source-selection argument | Alt text, caption, source line, expandable transcript |
| RSS timeline | Show that feed formats persisted while prominent consumer interfaces faded | Historical section | Alt text, caption, dated source line, expandable transcript |
| One feed, many consumers | Show current uses beyond blogs | Modern-use section | Alt text, caption, institutional source line, expandable transcript |
| 92-feed OPML lineage | Preserve the ranking, conversion, sharing, and downstream-use attribution chain | Karpathy and OPML section | Alt text, caption, source line, expandable transcript |

The vector treatment uses the existing HobFarm dark palette, orange feed line, cyan reader endpoints, restrained glow, live text, and no motion. The supplied illustration adds a paper-and-ink industrial treatment without replacing the shallow hero, which would crop away its reader destinations. Every body graphic remains understandable from its transcript if images do not load.
